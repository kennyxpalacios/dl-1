# dl-1 // positioning

the one line: stop using those sites. this one looks like it belongs on your desk.

naming: write "dl-1" in running text (people search with a hyphen). the en dash "dl–1" is the wordmark; use it on visuals, the way the panel renders it.

## three angles

01 // retire the sketchy site
line: no pop-ups. no fake download buttons. no "allow notifications." paste a link, press run.
why it lands: every creator has clicked the wrong download button at least once. nobody needs it explained, so it works in the first second, and it's already kenny's sentence.

02 // the reference folder
line: the moodboard, the reference clip in your timeline, the audio for the edit, your own posts before they vanish. one panel in your dock.
why it lands: it shows the work instead of naming it. creators see their own week in it, and the boundary (your stuff, references, licensed material) is built into the examples.

03 // a piece of gear
line: a downloader that looks like it belongs on your desk, not a tab you close before anyone sees it.
why it lands: design-minded creators choose tools partly on feel. the panel is the image that stops the scroll, and no sketchy site can copy it.

## the spine: 01

01 is the spine because the contrast with those sites is something everyone in the audience recognizes instantly, and it's kenny's own pitch. 03 is the proof and 02 is the content, so every piece opens on the contrast, shows the real uses, and closes on the object.

## say / don't say

01. say: paste a link. pick a format. press run. // don't say: game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, supercharge, unlock.
02. say: the reference folder, the clip for your timeline, the audio for the edit, your old posts. // don't say: "save your own work." show it instead.
03. say: your posts, your b-roll, references, licensed music. // don't say: download anyone's content, rip, steal, grab any video.
04. say: about a thousand sites. // don't say: every site, any video, works forever.
05. say: runs on your mac. nothing is uploaded. open source. no ads. // don't say: secure, virus-free, 100% safe, legal, private by design.
06. say: free. open source. mit license. no account, no watermark. // don't say: free forever, or anything about future pricing.
07. say: hardware-inspired. looks like gear on your desk. // don't say: any other company's name or product, "te-style," "inspired by [brand]."
08. on instagram / facebook, say: a link, the link you paste. // don't say: youtube, instagram, tiktok, facebook. not in copy, captions, alt text, audio or file names.
09. on x, hn, product hunt, say: built on yt-dlp + ffmpeg. // don't say: "youtube downloader" or "instagram downloader," anywhere.
10. say: h.264 mp4 that cuts clean in premiere, resolve, final cut. 320 kbps mp3. // don't say: 4k, lossless, best quality possible.
11. say: mac, macos 11+. apple silicon zip, intel from source. // don't say: one-click install (first launch needs "open anyway"), windows, any mac.
12. say: sites change, and yt-dlp has to keep up. // don't say: guaranteed, always works, never breaks.
